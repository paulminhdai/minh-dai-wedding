What should I wear?

Formal wedding attire. We warmly invite you to wear whatever makes you feel elegant and comfortable. Our wedding theme embraces dark blue/navy tones, and you are welcome to wear shades within this palette if you wish. Kindly avoid white or off-white tones.

What's the weather like in June?

June in Southern California is typically warm and sunny with temperatures in the 70s-80s°F. The ceremony is indoors, and the reception venue is climate-controlled.
